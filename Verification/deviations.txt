This is modelled on Cody Anderson's Procedures A and C submitted to the Fraunhoefer Round-Robin.

Notable deviations from his procedures include:

Closing rings, filling holes and watershedding is performed after the masks are combined, rather
than in the otsu stage. This is because particles that are separated by watershedding in the otsu
are more often than not rejoined in the DoG. Moving this stage ensures that watershedding is
applied to the full mask, not just one constituent mask, and likewise for ring closing and hole
filling.
